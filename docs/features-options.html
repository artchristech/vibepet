Design review, round 2: the pet in action. Feedback was to stop decorating and start demonstrating. Each feature is now a short loop where the pet reacts to real-looking events: an agent finishing, a diff growing, a double-click, a click passing through it. There's no copy to read; you watch it happen. Its eyes follow the on-screen cursor.
